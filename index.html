Perception of rearticulated and checked phonations in Yateé Zapotec

Identification tasks used in the study. Each page runs in the browser; audio plays after you click the start button.

  • Experiment I: six-alternative identification of [ja] words, with glottalization position, degree, and vowel duration manipulated.
  • Experiments II and III: two-alternative identification with minimal pairs, with glottalization position, degree, proportion, and vowel duration manipulated.
